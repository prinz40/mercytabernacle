Our Legacy of Faith

The Journey So Far

July 2007 — The Foundation

Surrender & Search

The journey began when our pastor surrendered his life to the lordship of Jesus. To grow in kingdom knowledge, he attended the Word of Faith Bible Institute (WOFBI) at Raji-Oba, Lagos, where mentorship from Bishop David Oyedepo sparked a deep quest to understand God's unique plan.

Sept 15, 2007 — Visionary Encounter

Isaiah 61 Revelation

Seeking clear divine direction, he embarked on a 5-day fast at Ojodu Berger, Lagos, studying Kenneth Hagin's blueprint. On the third day (September 18), the Spirit spoke directly through Isaiah 61. Later, a second confirmation came through Luke 4:18 during a midnight prayer altar in Lagos.

Sept 2012 — Prophetic Mandate

"Go Liberate the Land"

Returning to Benin City, he resolved to answer the call completely. In a vivid visionary encounter, the Spirit gave him Isaiah 44:13, charging him with specific instructions and landmarks concerning Ekpoma and Ukpenu to deliver captives free without price or reward.

Nov 25, 2012 — First Service

Commencement in a Parlour

Arriving in Ekpoma with just a bag, a Bible, a bell, an anointing oil bottle, and a megaphone, he anointed Ejodamen junction ground. Led to an abandoned building once used by local witch doctors, our first official service held on November 25, 2012 in a parlour—starting only with God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit!
